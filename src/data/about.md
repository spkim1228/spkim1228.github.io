Hello hello! Welcome to my site! My name is Paul and I'm currently pursuing independent AI alignment research. I graduated from [Williams College](https://www.williams.edu/) in 2024 with a B.A. in Computer Science and Psychology. I wrote my undergraduate [thesis](https://spkim1228.github.io/cv.pdf) with Prof. [Iris Howley](https://irishowley.academicwebsite.com/) on explainability methods via creating post-hoc “explainables” for TinyML systems.

I spent two years post-grad working as a research associate for [Max Miller](https://www.max-miller.finance/) at [Harvard Business School](https://www.hbs.edu/), focusing on finance, macroeconomics, and political economy.

I am currently interested in working on AI Safety and alignment research with a focus on understanding the mechanisms behind deviant and undesirable model behavior and mitigating such behavior through CoT monitoring and training.

## Research Interests

I am still figuring out my research interests, but they broadly include: **Interpretability**,  **Group Dynamics**, **Behavioral evaluation**, and **Human-AI Interaction**.
